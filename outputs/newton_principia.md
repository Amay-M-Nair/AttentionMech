### Def. I

**LA** Quantitas Materiae est mensura ejusdem orta ex illius Densitate et Magnitudine conjunctim.

**EN** The height of Materia is of the same measure from Denstia and Magnus.

### Def. II

**LA** Quantitas motus est mensura ejusdem orta ex Velocitate et quantitate Materiae conjunctim.

**EN** How great is the measure of the same effect, from Velocia and the fourfold connection of Materia.

**EN** The power of the Materians is inspired by the power of resisting, the body, and the whole body, which is in itself, had perished in its position, or to rest, or to move on the right.
